# 12 — Rendition composition

**What to build:** From a chosen angle and its confirmed claims, the system generates one X
thread — a rendition made of ordered units, each unit citing only confirmed claims,
generated natively for the platform rather than cut down from something longer.

**Blocked by:** 11 — Angle Room and claim confirmation

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Operator can trigger rendition generation for a chosen angle, targeting the X-thread
      format.
- [ ] The generated rendition is composed of ordered units.
- [ ] Every unit's cited claims are drawn only from the angle's confirmed claim set — an
      attempt to cite an unconfirmed claim is refused.
- [ ] Required qualification language attached to a cited claim is present in the unit that
      cites it.
- [ ] The governed operation is tested at the agreed seam, including the refusal case for
      citing an unconfirmed claim.
