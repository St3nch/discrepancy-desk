# The Discrepancy Desk

## North star

The Discrepancy Desk is a private investigative and research system for difficult,
disputed, strange, and culturally charged subjects. It preserves the available record,
separates what sources present from what the Desk claims, keeps competing explanations
visible, and produces accountable living investigative Files.

The Desk succeeds when it can gather substantial factual material, break a difficult
subject into inspectable questions, show what is well explained and what is not, and let
the audience retain judgment.

> The Desk does the research; the audience keeps the judgment.

A useful File may conclude that most evidence supports an ordinary explanation while
specific details remain unresolved. It may also show narrative growth, source dependence,
conflicting testimony, weak evidence, genuine strangeness, or several of those at once.
The Product does not require CHAZ, the Desk, or the audience to believe a subject before
investigating it.

## Product stance

A **Discrepancy** is something in the available Record that does not fit cleanly and
therefore earns investigation.

> A discrepancy earns investigation. It does not earn a conclusion.

The Desk is neither a belief engine nor a debunking engine. It may state interpretations,
but it must distinguish them from captured material, source-local presentation, competing
Claims, and human judgment. It does not manufacture certainty, consensus, or suspense.
Universal truth, trust, suspicion, and confidence scores are rejected.

Subjects may include conspiracy lore, disputed claims, weird history, UFO/UAP, QAnon,
alleged intelligence or shadow-government operations, NWO lore, anomalous events, and
other contested historical material. A subject category is navigation, not a verdict.

## Product shape

The Desk begins as a local, single-operator investigative workbench. Its durable unit of
attention is a **File**. A File remains open to revision as new evidence, corrections, and
better explanations arrive.

Ordinary notes, leads, questions, snippets, hypotheses, and story ideas belong to
**Workspace**. They do not become institutional investigative **Record** merely because
someone or some model wrote them down. Admission to Record is governed and traceable.

The current internal Product path is:

```text
File
  → Capture / Artifact / Surface / Locator
  → Observation
  → Claim
  → human Decision
  → File-scoped Discrepancy
  → living internal report
  → exact evidence walkback
```

Future public articles, posts, scripts, videos, and pages are Renditions of governed File
material. They are not alternate investigations or truth stores.

## Evidence and reasoning doctrine

The evidence vocabulary is media-neutral:

```text
Capture → Artifact → Surface → Locator → Observation
```

- A Capture records a governed acquisition.
- An Artifact preserves the acquired source material.
- A Surface is a frozen representation used for inspection and citation.
- A Locator addresses an exact place or range in one exact Surface or Artifact version.
- An Observation records what that source presents, not whether the proposition is true.

Original document, image, audio, or video material remains evidence authority. OCR,
transcripts, extracted frames, normalization, and enhancement are derived Surfaces with
explicit lineage. They never silently replace the original or inherit its evidentiary
authority.

A Claim is a Desk-level proposition. Evidence may support or contradict it. A human
Decision acts on an exact durable target or version. Observation, Claim, and Decision
must not collapse into one convenient truth field.

Source disagreement, uncertainty, incomplete custody, retrieval limitations, temporal
precision, and ordinary alternative explanations remain visible. A working URL is an
acquisition route, not proof of origin, authenticity, completeness, independence, or
reuse rights.

Research should distinguish contemporaneous material from later retelling, identify
source genealogy where possible, test the strongest ordinary explanations, and state
what the available corpus cannot establish. Research volume is not corroboration.

## Authority

CHAZ is Product Owner and final authority for Product direction, priorities,
consequential choices, provider or spend authorization, publication, release, and push.

Codex is the VedaOps Project Steward. The Steward maintains the complete project model,
reconciles Product and architecture, sequences work, maintains authority, prepares
bounded assignments, independently reviews results, and accepts or rejects work against
the repository.

Claude, Grok, and other capable models may research, review, design, implement, or test
bounded work. Model availability changes assignments, not project authority. No other
model receives or assumes the Project Steward role. Agent output is working input until
the Steward verifies and reconciles it; consequential Product choices remain CHAZ's.

## Presentation and publication

The living File report is the current canonical projection of what the Desk understands.
It may change through governed lineage.

A Publication preserves the exact human-authorized content released at that time. Later
Record changes never silently rewrite public history. Material factual, assessment,
discrepancy, context, or correction changes require authorization of the exact revised
content.

Quinton Clearance is a fictional public-facing clerk and presenter:

> The Desk investigates. Quinton presents the files.

Quinton does not Capture evidence, create Observations or Claims, make Decisions, or alter
Record.

## Survival and change

Structured Record state is authoritative in PostgreSQL 18. Immutable evidence payload
authority remains a distinct Vault concern. Both must preserve provenance and version
history so the Desk can reconstruct what it had, understood, decided, and published at an
earlier point without rewriting the past.

Rebuildable indexes, projections, summaries, and search aids may improve access. Nothing
durable may depend only on rebuildable state.

## Current milestone

The PostgreSQL 18 foundation checkpoint is complete. It proved selected database
behaviors without creating a production schema or authorizing another foundation
program.

The active milestone is FILE-01: the first real investigative File, `DD-7225`, concerning
the Rendlesham Forest incident of December 1980. It must use real document and audio
material to prove the minimum path from Capture to an operator-readable living report
with exact evidence walkback.

The working investigation question is:

> What does the contemporaneous record establish about the Rendlesham Forest incident,
> how did the story change in later retellings, and which reported details remain
> unexplained after the strongest conventional explanations are considered?

Strategy Layer is outside this Product milestone. The Desk is built according to its own
needs and earns future outward work by first producing a real investigative Record.

## Working restraint

Build the smallest honest end-to-end Product slice that real material can falsify.

> Code the scaffold. Do not code the taste.

Do not substitute a website, autonomous newsroom, giant notebook, universal ontology,
graph-as-truth system, broad media-processing platform, or another infrastructure program
for completing a real File.
