# Discrepancy Desk Deferred Register

Deferred means deliberately unbuilt. An entry preserves intent, trigger, and cost of
forgetting; it does not smuggle a design or ticket into the backlog.

## F1 — Research source and method library

**Direction:** The Desk should accumulate useful websites, archives, catalogs, books,
investigative methods, access constraints, and source-specific lessons so later Files do
not rediscover the same research terrain.

**Why not now:** FILE-01 must first expose what information is actually reusable:
institutional source identity, acquisition routes, search tactics, access limitations,
credibility or custody cautions, and topic-specific versus general research value.

**Trigger:** The Rendlesham investigation produces repeated source-discovery knowledge
that would materially improve another research pass or File.

**Cost of forgetting:** Research becomes chat memory and repeated browsing, while weak
mirrors or secondary summaries are mistaken for source authority.

## F2 — Broad Workspace and notebook experience

**Direction:** The Desk will eventually need comfortable support for notes, leads,
questions, todos, snippets, hypotheses, and story ideas.

**Why not now:** FILE-01 needs only enough operator workflow to complete one real File.
Workspace must not become Record by convenience.

**Trigger:** Real investigation is materially slowed by handling ordinary working
material outside the Product.

**Cost of forgetting:** Researchers create scattered shadow records or pressure the Record
model to absorb ungoverned notes.

## F3 — General OCR and document-processing platform

**Direction:** Documents and images may benefit from OCR, layout extraction, region
detection, and improved inspection Surfaces.

**Why not now:** FILE-01 needs only bounded derived Surfaces for material it actually cites.

**Trigger:** Repeated real captures require the same processing and manual treatment is
materially limiting research.

**Cost of forgetting:** Ad hoc extractor output may become uncited pseudo-authority.

## F4 — General audio/video processing and enhancement

**Direction:** Audio and video may eventually support transcription, speaker/time
segmentation, frame extraction, image-region location, comparison, and carefully labelled
enhancement.

**Why not now:** FILE-01 requires only the minimum time-coded audio Surface needed for
representative Observations. Video is not required.

**Trigger:** A real File needs repeatable processing beyond the bounded FILE-01 path.

**Cost of forgetting:** Transcripts, frames, or enhancements may detach from authoritative
original media and their exact lineage.

## F5 — Source genealogy and narrative-mutation analysis

**Direction:** The Desk should identify reprints, near-clones, quote inheritance, common
origin, qualifier loss, wording changes, added actors or motives, and precision that
appears without new evidence.

**Why not now:** One File can preserve explicit source dependence manually. Broad reusable
machinery needs repeated examples.

**Trigger:** Multiple sources or Files make independence and retelling lineage a recurring
investigative question.

**Cost of forgetting:** Source volume is mistaken for independent corroboration and later
retellings quietly overwrite earlier testimony.

## F6 — General Entity identity resolution

**Direction:** Identity resolution must eventually support reversible merge, split, and
conflict history without rewriting evidence provenance.

**Why not now:** FILE-01 does not require a general Entity system unless its real corpus
proves otherwise.

**Trigger:** A File cannot honestly connect or distinguish people, organizations, places,
or aliases without durable governed resolution.

**Cost of forgetting:** Names become accidental identity, or later correction requires
destructive rewriting.

## F7 — Broad search, graph, and semantic retrieval

**Direction:** Full-text search, embeddings, graph projections, and bounded semantic
retrieval may improve discovery over accumulated Record.

**Why not now:** They are rebuildable access aids, not Record authority, and one File does
not justify a platform.

**Trigger:** Measured investigation or evidence-walkback work becomes materially difficult
with direct governed reads.

**Cost of forgetting:** Operators may build ungoverned shadow indexes or confuse a
similarity result with evidence.

## F8 — Autonomous research and scheduled monitoring

**Direction:** Models may eventually help discover candidates, monitor sources, and
perform bounded recurring research.

**Why not now:** Authority, scope, spend, capture, source safety, and admission behavior
must first be proven through human-directed real work.

**Trigger:** Repeated Files establish a bounded research operation whose inputs, outputs,
refusals, and human checkpoints can be stated precisely.

**Cost of forgetting:** Valuable sources are missed; premature implementation instead
creates an unsupervised content harvester.

## F9 — Public website and publication workflow

**Direction:** The Desk may publish canonical File pages and other exact
human-authorized Renditions with immutable Publication history and corrections.

**Why not now:** The investigative Record and living internal report must become real
before public presentation is designed.

**Trigger:** A File contains material worth showing and CHAZ chooses an outward artifact.

**Cost of forgetting:** Internal structure may make accurate publication unnecessarily
difficult; building early would optimize an empty display case.

## F10 — Quinton content production

**Direction:** Quinton Clearance may present File material in articles, posts, scripts,
videos, and other target-native Renditions.

**Why not now:** Quinton requires governed File material and a real editorial need.

**Trigger:** CHAZ selects a File and format for outward presentation.

**Cost of forgetting:** Public voice may be improvised without exact Claim/evidence
binding; building early invites Quinton into investigation.

## F11 — X and other social acquisition or publishing

**Direction:** Official APIs and bounded manual capture may eventually acquire social
material; exact approved Renditions may later be published and measured.

**Why not now:** No current File or publication requires platform integration.

**Trigger:** A real investigation needs social testimony or an approved outward plan
requires a specific platform operation.

**Cost of forgetting:** Social evidence may be captured inconsistently; building early
adds provider, spend, terms, and publication risk without Product work.

## F12 — Public File-number allocation mechanism

**Direction:** Future File IDs must remain collision-free, non-semantic, and
non-sequential without implying archive size or chronology.

**Why not now:** `DD-7225` is selected and only one File exists.

**Trigger:** Before assigning the second public File ID.

**Cost of forgetting:** Operators may increment the prior number, collide with an existing
File, or leak real creation order.

## F13 — Multi-user, remote, and production access

**Direction:** Authentication, authorization, multi-user editing, remote service exposure,
and hosted operations may eventually be required.

**Why not now:** The Desk is a local single-operator Product and FILE-01 does not require a
network service.

**Trigger:** A second human operator or non-local production client needs governed access.

**Cost of forgetting:** A local trust boundary could be exposed as though it were
production-safe.

## F14 — Additional acquisition providers and automation

**Direction:** Archives, official APIs, web capture tools, and specialist providers may
eventually support repeatable acquisition.

**Why not now:** FILE-01 uses a deliberately small manually verified corpus. The existence
of a tool or provider does not create a Product requirement.

**Trigger:** A concrete source cannot be captured faithfully through the accepted path, or
repeated manual acquisition creates demonstrated cost or risk.

**Cost of forgetting:** Valuable evidence may remain inaccessible; premature adapters
would create infrastructure without investigative demand.
